Our Mission

Engineering Nepal's Digital Grid

Nepal sits on one of the world's greatest untapped energy reserves — 42,000 MW of economically viable hydropower. Yet millions still face load shedding, billing inaccuracies, and grid instability.

DAFEGEN exists to close this gap. We bring world-class energy software to Nepal's utilities and distribution companies, enabling them to serve every consumer with the accuracy, reliability, and intelligence they deserve.

Our vision: a digitally connected, efficiently managed energy system that powers Nepal's economic transformation — from hydropower generation to the last meter in the most remote village.
